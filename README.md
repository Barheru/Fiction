## Hello Hello!

This is my fiction repository. I do not like making people download pdfs in order to read my work, and I dislike putting any personal writing in Google Drive these days. 

Please note that many of these pieces have content warnings. If I share a piece with you that links to this GitHub, I will share the content warnings with you. If you are merely browsing this repository, please be aware that these pieces may contain strong themes of body horror, memory loss, and religious abuse, trauma, and symbolism. Proceed accordingly.

These pieces are often contextual to the communities that help tell them. Pieces in the Space Station 14 folder are told with other players on SS14 servers. These pieces are often retrospectives and in between scenes. No context is promised nor given.

Space is wide and good friends are too few!
